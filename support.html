Getting started

Open Nuclear TV and choose Add provider login (server address, username and password from your TV provider) or Add M3U playlist (a playlist link, and optionally a TV guide link). Your library appears once it has loaded; large libraries take a minute the first time.

Common questions

Where do I get channels or a subscription? From a TV provider of your choice. Nuclear TV is a player only and doesn't sell or recommend services.

Something won't play. Try again, or pick another player for that video from the player's menu. If your plan allows a limited number of connections, stop playback on your other devices first.

I see languages or categories I don't want. Settings → Languages & Categories hides them for the current profile.

Adult content. It's hidden by default. Settings → Parental Controls → Show Adult Content turns it on for one profile after an 18+ notice and the household PIN.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/h1vprjcs/nuclear-tv/issues. Please don't include your provider login, server address or playlist links.
