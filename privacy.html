Nuclear TV

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Nuclear TV is a video player for TV services you already subscribe to. It has no accounts with us, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We (the developer) don't receive any of your data.

What stays on your device

  • Your provider logins and playlist links are stored in your device's Keychain.
  • Settings, profiles, My List, watch history and resume points are stored on your device only.
  • A library cache (channel, movie and show lists, the TV guide, artwork) is kept in the app's cache folder so the app opens quickly. It can be deleted at any time and is rebuilt from your provider.
  • A small diagnostic log on your device, which never contains logins, server addresses or stream links.

Who the app talks to

  • Your TV provider's server, the one you entered, to load your channels, movies, shows and guide, and to play them. What that server does is covered by your provider's own policy.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database), to show artwork, descriptions, cast and ratings. The app sends title names, years and TMDB ids, never your logins. Adult titles are never sent. See TMDB's privacy policy.
  • YouTube, only when you choose to watch a trailer: it opens in YouTube.

Nothing the app sends to these services identifies you: no name, email, device identifier, account or location. We don't sell, share or transfer personal data to anyone, because we don't collect any.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Everything stays on your device until you delete it. We never receive a copy, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete on our side.
  • Remove an account (Settings → Accounts) to delete its saved login from the Keychain and its library from the cache.
  • Delete a profile (Settings → Profiles) to delete its My List, history, resume points and recent searches.
  • Delete the app to remove its data from the device. Remove your accounts first if you also want the saved logins gone from the Keychain.

Children

Nuclear TV has Kids profiles that show only titles rated for children. Adult content from a provider is hidden on every profile unless someone turns it on with the household PIN. Nuclear TV doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
