Nuclear TV

Your TV service, beautifully organised.

Nuclear TV is a player for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Add the login or playlist your own TV provider gave you, and it turns your movies, shows and live channels into a fast, tidy library with a TV guide, Continue Watching, My List, Kids profiles and parental controls.

Nuclear TV doesn't come with any content.

It plays only the services you already subscribe to. It doesn't sell subscriptions, include channels or suggest where to find them.
